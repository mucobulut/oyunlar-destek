Frequently asked questions

How do I remove ads and unlock everything?

Open the parents' area (lock icon), answer the parental question and choose the monthly Premium subscription (all levels and packs, no ads) or a one-time purchase.

I bought something but it is locked.

Open the parents' area and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID / Huawei ID.

How do I cancel the subscription?

iPhone/iPad: Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Huawei: AppGallery › Me › Subscriptions.

How do I turn the sound off?

Tap the speaker button on the main menu, map or pause screen. The game remembers your choice.
